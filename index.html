Principal Investigator · Nathaniel D.M. Jenkins, PhD, FAHA

Stress leaves a signature in the vasculature.

How do psychosocial and lifestyle exposures program the integrated vascular, metabolic, and physiological responses that determine cardiovascular resilience or vulnerability across the lifespan?

Mission

The VSPR Lab studies how psychosocial and environmental stressors affect blood vessel health across the lifespan, combining vascular physiology, cellular and mitochondrial biology, and behavioral observation and manipulation to uncover what drives cardiometabolic disease risk in high-risk populations.

Sleep, diet, and physical activity are measured as exposures that shape vascular and metabolic function, and manipulated experimentally to test causal mechanisms in humans.

Using tools like live-cell imaging and mitochondrial phenotyping, the lab traces how stress and other circulating factors act on endothelial and mitochondrial function. That work connects everyday biological stress to long-term vascular and metabolic disease.
